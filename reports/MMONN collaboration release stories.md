# MMONN collaborations connect six shared releases

Research supports three-sentence descriptions for all six collaborations using official artist uploads, distributor metadata and streaming listings. The copy below is ready for the scrolling portfolio section. Song themes are paraphrased only where artist-published lyrics establish them; other entries use verified release and credit details. No recording-session stories, popularity claims or personal-life interpretations are added. Sources remain outside the public descriptions so they can be retained as an editorial audit trail. Research checked November 25, 2026.

## Lason joins three artists on Memento Mori

Lason brings MMONN together with Alisson Shore and Because on a three-artist hip-hop release. First released as a single on September 14, 2025, it also appears as the second track on Alisson Shore’s Memento Mori. The collaboration sits within an album that explores love, loss and transformation.

Sources: [Apple Music single](https://music.apple.com/us/album/lason-single/1821387337), [Spotify album](https://open.spotify.com/embed/album/5czFvng5qXaj4ldZ3exB4K), [Alisson Shore’s album context](https://alissonshore.com/).

## PLY4 returns with DEMI in 2025

MMONN revisits PLY4 with DEMI, turning his original 2023 solo release into a shared record. Released in June 2025, this version is written and performed by both artists, with production credited to CRMDRM. Plumberboi handles the arrangement, mixing and mastering, while DEMI’s official video presents the collaboration as PLY4 (Remix).

Sources: [Original 2023 single](https://music.apple.com/us/album/ply4-single/1694911181), [Sony-delivered official audio](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Urz4D1c8twA), [DEMI’s official remix video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tfn6vTm8394).

## Chillerong Busy balances work with rest

Released in September 2023, Chillerong Busy brings MMONN and Lanzeta together around the push and pull of everyday work. Its lyrics balance ambition with rest, moving between the pressure to keep going and the need to slow down. The song makes room for family, personal goals and a calmer pace without giving up on the work ahead.

Sources: [Distributor audio and release date](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MMpyeIcEXvI), [MMONN’s official visualizer and lyrics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ITUJQ1b4R2U).

## Pagod Na traces exhaustion and separation

Pagod Na pairs MMONN with RB Slatt on a single released in November 2022. Across their verses, the two voices trace a relationship worn down by hurt and the exhaustion of holding on. The song moves toward separation and letting go, giving both performers a place in its account of heartbreak.

Sources: [Apple Music single](https://music.apple.com/us/album/pagod-na-feat-rb-slatt-single/1687579702), [MMONN’s official audio and lyrics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NTSZx_4lFe0).

## Kung Kakapit Ka features Ne7in

Released on December 27, 2024, Kung Kakapit Ka pairs MMONN with featured artist Ne7in. The R&B/Soul single unfolds over three minutes and 52 seconds. Its official lyric video accompanies the release with visuals credited to Orcaworldwide.

Sources: [Apple Music single](https://music.apple.com/us/album/kung-kakapit-ka-feat-ne7in-single/1786919206), [Amazon Music duration](https://music.amazon.com/tracks/B0DR7Y87FQ), [Official lyric video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nRFoGtV7-Vc).

## Amarad adds kyleaux to the catalogue

Amarad brings MMONN and featured artist kyleaux together on an R&B single released in September 2026. Issued on the MMONN label, the track runs for three minutes and 24 seconds. Its release credits name Mmonen Moreno as both producer and composer.

Sources: [Qobuz release and credits](https://www.qobuz.com/au-en/album/amarad-mmonn/tp3d2x72p7ott), [Apple Music artist release listing](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/mmonn/1632799825).

## Credit corrections and unresolved details

**Alisson Shore** is the spelling used by release listings. The existing PLY4 collaboration video points to MMONN’s original; DEMI’s official remix is `tfn6vTm8394`. June 2025 is used in the copy because song-level listings differ between June 21 and June 27, although Sony-delivered audio and the album listing support June 27. ([Official remix video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tfn6vTm8394), [Sony-delivered audio](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Urz4D1c8twA), [Apple Music album](https://music.apple.com/us/album/ply4-single/1821157432))

**Kung Kakapit Ka is officially listed as MMONN featuring Ne7in**. SXBER appears in owner-authored website content but was not found in the checked official metadata; an additional contribution should be confirmed with the owner before it is presented as a release credit. The recommended description therefore names Ne7in. ([Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/kung-kakapit-ka-feat-ne7in-single/1786919206), [Official lyric video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nRFoGtV7-Vc))

Pagod Na has inconsistent producer naming between MMONN’s official upload and LOCAL’s promotional upload, so its public description omits production credit. Amarad’s distributor metadata identifies Mmonen Moreno, but this report does not equate that credit with another artist identity. Some official YouTube descriptions were available through indexed search after direct access was throttled. ([MMONN’s upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NTSZx_4lFe0), [LOCAL upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HqOCy4KA1CE), [Qobuz](https://www.qobuz.com/au-en/album/amarad-mmonn/tp3d2x72p7ott))

Those owner-provided facts can remain owner-authored biography; they should not be described as independently corroborated by this research.
