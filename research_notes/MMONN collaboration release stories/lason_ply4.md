# Lason and PLY4 Remix

## What is verified about Lason?

### Takeaway
The collaboration is credited to **Alisson Shore**, Because and MMONN, and was released as a single on September 14, 2025. It also appears as track two on Alisson Shore's Memento Mori album.

### Cited Findings
- Apple Music single page credits Alisson Shore, Because and MMONN, lists Hip-Hop/Rap and September 14, 2025, with phonogram copyright Alisson Shore. [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/lason-single/1821387337)
- Spotify's actual track used in the site's embed, `1IZkg1yKs8wQOwXZveRkRN`, belongs to Memento Mori, runs 3:03 and has album date October 19, 2025. [Spotify track](https://open.spotify.com/track/1IZkg1yKs8wQOwXZveRkRN)
- The album's track list places Lason second and credits all three artists. [Spotify album](https://open.spotify.com/embed/album/5czFvng5qXaj4ldZ3exB4K)
- Alisson Shore's official video was published September 14, 2025, titled Alisson Shore - Lason (ft. Because, MMONN); its description announces Memento Mori for October 19. [Official artist video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s6W62wMUsVk)
- Distributor-generated official artist audio credits Alisson Shore as sound engineer and composer, Emmanuel Sambayan as lyricist. Do not assume these are comprehensive performer/writer credits. [Official audio](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LDTsWn9LlMA)
- Artist's official store describes Memento Mori as a cinematic concept album about love, loss, transformation, passion, heartbreak, depression, addiction and rebirth. This is album-level context, not an artist explanation of Lason specifically. [Artist website](https://alissonshore.com/)

### Inferences
Suggested public copy, three sentences (no invented song story):
“Lason brings MMONN together with Alisson Shore and Because on a three-artist hip-hop release. First issued as a single on September 14, 2025, the track also appears on Alisson Shore’s Memento Mori. Its place as the album’s second track connects this collaboration to a larger body of work built around love, loss and transformation.”
The final sentence combines verified track placement and the artist's album description; it does not claim a specific Lason lyric interpretation.

### Gaps
- Existing site spelling “Alison Shore” is incorrect; sources consistently use **Alisson Shore**.
- There is also a 2024 MMONN release named Lason in catalog listings; do not confuse it with this 2025 three-artist collaboration.
- No reliable interview explaining the collaboration's origin was found. Do not fabricate studio meetings, personal stories or chart impact.

## What is verified about PLY4 Remix?

### Takeaway
DEMI and MMONN's joint 2025 release is titled PLY4 on streaming services and PLY4 (Remix) on DEMI's official video. It follows MMONN's original 2023 PLY4 and has verified production and writing credits.

### Cited Findings
- Apple Music album page lists PLY4 by DEMI and MMONN as R&B/Soul, released June 27, 2025, exclusively distributed by Sony Music Entertainment Philippines. [Apple Music album](https://music.apple.com/us/album/ply4-single/1821157432)
- Sony-delivered official artist audio explicitly lists release date June 27, 2025; associated performers DEMI and MMONN; producer CRMDRM; arranger, mastering and mixing engineer Plumberboi; composers Demi Chelsy Malto, Mmonen Khien Agdoro and Jianluigi Masongsong. [Official distributor audio](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Urz4D1c8twA)
- DEMI's official video calls it PLY4 (Remix), published June 29, 2025. Credits name MMONN and DEMI as writers and performers, Plumberboi for mixing/mastering, Joju for filming and CHEESE for lyric video. [Official artist video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tfn6vTm8394)
- MMONN's original solo Ply4 single was released July 1, 2023. [Original single on Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/ply4-single/1694911181)
- The original official audio title credits production to CRMDRM. [MMONN official audio](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=45HSCKGN6Do)
- Billboard Philippines independently refers to the DEMI/MMONN collaboration as a June 27 release. [Billboard Philippines](https://billboardphilippines.com/music/news/young-cocoa-demi-new-single-cheap-thrills-2025/)

### Inferences
Suggested public copy, three sentences:
“MMONN revisits PLY4 with DEMI, expanding the song from its original 2023 solo release into a shared record. Released in June 2025, the collaboration is written and performed by both artists, with production credited to CRMDRM. Plumberboi handles the arrangement, mixing and mastering, while DEMI’s official video presents this version as PLY4 (Remix).”
The first sentence is a conservative synthesis of the original solo credit and the explicitly labeled official remix, not an unverified claim about how the collaboration came about.

### Gaps
- Date conflict: Apple Music song page and Amazon track page show June 21, 2025, but album pages show June 27. Sony-delivered YouTube credits and Billboard corroborate June 27. Recommended public copy uses “June 2025” to avoid needless inconsistency.
- Existing site's YouTube embed `i2igE_cixyw` is the original MMONN video, **not** DEMI's remix. Correct remix video is `tfn6vTm8394`.
- No verified interview discussing how the artists met or conceived the remix was found; avoid inventing it.
